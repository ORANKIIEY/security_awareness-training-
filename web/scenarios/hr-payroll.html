02 · HR / payroll bonus

Confirm direct deposit today to release a bonus — or risk forfeiture. Find the manipulation before you reveal.

Annotated red flags

  1. 1
    Lookalike HR portal domain — not the real corporate mail domain.
  2. 2
    Different Reply-To — secure-acmebenefits.com is another host entirely.
  3. 3
    Generic greeting — “Dear Employee” for a personal bonus claim.
  4. 4
    Reward + deadline — bonus language stacked with “TODAY.”
  5. 5
    Forfeiture threat — loss aversion on money you “already earned.”
  6. 6
    Unexpected deposit form link — real changes go through known HRIS/SSO.
  7. 7
    “Confidential” — discourages verifying with a colleague or manager.

Psychology

Technique Principle Why it works
Bonus payout Curiosity / reward Positive outcomes pull clicks as hard as threats push them.
Same-day / Friday payroll Urgency Payday timing makes delay feel costly.
Forfeiture language Loss aversion People fight to keep a promised gain.
Payroll authority Authority HR controls pay; process requests feel mandatory.
