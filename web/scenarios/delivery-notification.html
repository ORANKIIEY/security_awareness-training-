Annotated red flags

  1. 1
    Hyphen-stuffed “secure” domain — lookalike logistics branding.
  2. 2
    Typosquat Reply-To — parcelink vs parcel-link.
  3. 3
    Generic greeting — no personal name or real order context.
  4. 4
    12-hour deadline — artificial urgency.
  5. 5
    Unexpected tracking ID — you didn’t order this (or can’t verify it).
  6. 6
    Fee / reschedule CTA — harvest pretext disguised as logistics.
  7. 7
    Wrong hover URL — not a real carrier host.
  8. 8
    “Do not reply” — funnels you only to the malicious link.

Psychology

Technique Principle Why it works
Package returned to sender Loss aversion People hate losing a parcel they believe is theirs.
12-hour window Urgency Encourages immediate mobile taps.
Tracking jargon Familiarity Mundane logistics language feels trustworthy.
Automated system tone Authority “Systems” feel official and harder to question.
