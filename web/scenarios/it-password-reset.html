Annotated red flags

  1. 1
    Lookalike domain — acmecorp-secure.net is not the real company domain.
  2. 2
    Mismatched Reply-To — replies steered to another attacker-controlled host.
  3. 3
    Generic greeting — “Dear User” instead of your name.
  4. 4
    Urgency + threat — 24-hour suspension language short-circuits scrutiny.
  5. 5
    Dead / wrong link — destination is not corporate SSO (training placeholder).
  6. 6
    Unsolicited “unusual activity” — cold verify-or-lose pretext.
  7. 7
    Authority costume — Information Security signature borrows real-team trust.

Psychology

Technique Principle Why it works
24-hour suspension Urgency Pushes reactive decisions over careful checking.
IT / InfoSec branding Authority People comply with security requests by default.
Loss of email access Fear / loss aversion Threats of loss hit harder than neutral reminders.
Subtle -secure domain Cognitive load Display names get skimmed; domains often aren’t.
