Annotated red flags

  1. 1
    Lookalike finance domain — acme-corp-finance.co is not corporate mail.
  2. 2
    Vendor-looking Reply-To — payment thread steered off-network.
  3. 3
    Executive pressure + busyness — authority costume of a rushed CFO.
  4. 4
    Same-day urgency — skips dual-control time.
  5. 5
    New bank details — classic BEC: change payee outside verified channels.
  6. 6
    Secrecy / don’t loop thread — blocks call-backs and dual review.
  7. 7
    Unexpected invoice link — verify before opening or paying.
  8. 8
    Mobile sign-off realism — familiarity cue that can lower suspicion.

Psychology

Technique Principle Why it works
CFO title Authority Staff hesitate to challenge senior leaders.
Wire today Urgency Process controls get skipped “just this once.”
Keep it quiet Fear / social pressure Isolates the target from colleagues who would spot the scam.
Invoice + AP routine Familiarity Mundane payment workflows feel safe.
