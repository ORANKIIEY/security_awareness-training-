# How to catch a phish — reusable checklist

**TRAINING EXAMPLE — DO NOT SEND.**

Use this on any suspicious email. You do not need to memorise every scenario — run the checklist.

## Before you click anything

1. **Check the real sender domain**, not just the display name.  
   Display names are trivial to fake. Expand the full address and ask: does the domain match the organisation that supposedly sent this?

2. **Hover over links (don’t click)** to see the real destination URL.  
   Look for lookalike domains, extra words (`-secure`, `-login`), wrong TLDs, or shortened URLs that hide the destination.

3. **Be suspicious of generic greetings** on mail that claims to know you.  
   “Dear User,” “Dear Customer,” or “Dear Employee” from “your” IT/HR/bank is a classic tell. Legitimate internal mail usually uses your name.

4. **Treat urgency and threats as a red flag in themselves.**  
   “24 hours,” “immediate suspension,” “final notice,” “wire today” — legitimate teams rarely demand irreversible action under extreme time pressure via email alone.

5. **Verify through a separate, known channel.**  
   Call IT/HR/finance/the bank using a number or portal you already trust. Do **not** use phone numbers or “support” links from the suspicious email.

6. **Check spelling, grammar, and tone.**  
   Not every phish has typos — but odd phrasing, mismatched branding language, or a tone that doesn’t match how that team usually writes is worth a pause.

7. **Inspect Reply-To and unexpected attachments.**  
   A friendly From line with a different Reply-To, or an unexpected invoice/PDF/ZIP you weren’t expecting, deserves extra scrutiny.

8. **When in doubt, report — don’t click or ignore.**  
   Forward to your security/IT reporting channel (or use the “Report phishing” button). Ignoring it leaves the next person exposed; reporting helps the organisation respond.

## Quick mental model

| Pressure you feel | Pause and ask |
| --- | --- |
| Fear of losing access / money | Would they really cut me off with one email and one link? |
| Authority (“IT / CEO / bank”) | Can I confirm this via a channel I already trust? |
| Curiosity / reward (“refund,” “prize,” “package”) | Did I initiate this? Is the domain real? |
| Busy / scanning fast | What would I notice if I slowed down for 10 seconds? |
